# Google Merchandise Store — Digital Analytics Case Study

**GA4 · Looker Studio · E-commerce Analytics · Funnel Analysis · Growth Strategy**

An end-to-end digital analytics case study built to understand **what drives revenue, where customers drop off, and where growth opportunities exist**.

### Business Problem

Revenue increased while active users declined. The objective was to identify the **drivers of growth, customer behavior, funnel leakage, product performance, and marketing efficiency**.

### Analytical Approach

**Data → KPI Analysis → Segmentation → Funnel Diagnosis → Root-Cause Hypotheses → Recommendations**

The analysis covers:

* YoY revenue & user performance
* Conversion and revenue efficiency
* New vs. returning customers
* Product & category performance
* Purchase funnel drop-offs
* Acquisition channels & ROAS
* Geographic and behavioral patterns
* Data quality & tracking gaps

### Key Findings

**+13.9% Revenue**
despite **−8.5% Active Users**

Growth was primarily associated with improved monetization efficiency, stronger returning-user economics, and high-performing products and channels.

The funnel also revealed significant leakage between **product discovery → cart** and **checkout → purchase**, highlighting clear optimization opportunities.

### Business Outcome

Translated GA4 data into **actionable insights, growth opportunities, and measurable recommendations** across acquisition, conversion, retention, and product strategy.
